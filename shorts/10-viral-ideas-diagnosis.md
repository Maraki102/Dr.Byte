# Dr. Byte – 10 viral θέματα (κοινό 25–45, φόρμουλα DIAGNOSIS + loop)

Δομή κάθε βίντεο (25–30" = 2 κλιπ, ένας ομιλητής ανά κλιπ):
0" HOOK (κραυγή/σοκ) → χτύπημα κάθε 4–5" → PUNCHLINE → ερώτηση στον θεατή → LOOP.
Ετικέτα: 🩺 DIAGNOSIS: … · Τέλος: 📲 Send this to …

1. QUICK-QUESTION TRAUMA – Hook: "My boss emailed me at 3:47 AM: 'Quick question.'" · Dr. Byte: "The two most dangerous words in human history. Nothing quick has EVER followed them." · Punch: "Treatment: airplane mode. Until Monday. Or retirement." · Loop: new buzz. · Q: "What time did YOUR boss send the worst one?" · Send: the coworker who answers emails at midnight.
2. VOICE-MESSAGE POISONING – Hook: "My mom sent a SEVEN-MINUTE voice message!" · Dr. Byte plays it 2x… 5x… 10x, hair standing up · Punch: "Summary: 'Call me.' Seven minutes. Two words. And the weather report." · Loop: new voice message, 9:12. · Q: "Longest voice message you ever got?" · Send: the friend who sends podcasts instead of texts.
3. GROUP-CHAT TRIP SYNDROME – Hook: "We've been planning Ibiza since 2019!" · Dr. Byte scans: "4,000 messages. 87 memes. Zero flights." · Punch: "That's not a trip. That's a hobby." · Loop: chat buzzes: "so… next year?" · Q: "How long has YOUR group been planning?" · Send: your group chat.
4. CHRONIC MONDAYITIS – Hook (eating pizza in gym clothes): "I start Monday!" · Dr. Byte unrolls a calendar with 47 crossed-out Mondays · Punch: "You've started 47 times. You're the most consistent beginner I've ever met." · Loop: "Okay. NEXT Monday." · Q: "What are YOU starting on Monday?" · Send: your 'I start Monday' friend.
5. 2 AM CART SYNDROME – Hook: doorbell, 14 boxes fall on the patient: "I don't remember ordering ANY of this!" · Dr. Byte unboxes: "A heated toilet seat. Three air fryers. And a book: 'How to Stop Impulse Buying.'" · Punch: "The book is also unread." · Loop: doorbell again. · Q: "Weirdest thing you bought at 2 AM?" · Send: the friend whose doorman knows them by name.
6. VACATION EMAIL DISORDER – Hook (beach chair, laptop): "I'm on vacation! I'm just checking ONE email." · Dr. Byte: "You checked 212. You replied 'Thanks!' to a newsletter." · Punch: buries the laptop in sand: "Out of office means OUT. OF. OFFICE." · Loop: patient digs it back up. · Q: "Do you check emails on vacation? Be honest." · Send: the colleague who's 'just quickly online'.
7. ADULT FRIENDSHIP SYNDROME – Hook: "We should DEFINITELY hang out soon!" · Dr. Byte checks both calendars: "Next date you're both free: March 2028." · Punch: "Symptoms: love each other deeply. Never meet. Ever." · Loop: "Let's catch up soon!" · Q: "How long since you saw your best friend?" · Send: the friend you've been 'catching up with soon' for 3 years.
8. 3 AM CRINGE REPLAY – Hook (exhausted in bed, eyes snap open): "Why do I remember THIS?!" · Dr. Byte's hologram replays: patient says "you too!" to a waiter who said "enjoy your meal" (2009) · Punch: "Your brain has one playlist. It's called 'Cringe'. It's on repeat. And it has no skip button." · Loop: patient lies down… brain plays the next one. · Q: "What's YOUR 3 AM cringe memory?" · Send: the friend who knows all your cringe stories.
9. FOUR-WORD PANIC – Hook: "My partner texted: 'We need to talk.'" · Dr. Byte: red alarm, CODE RED, packs an emergency bag · Phone buzzes: "…can you buy milk?" · Punch: "Four words. Three hours of panic. One litre of milk." · Loop: new text: "Also… we need to talk." · Q: "Scariest text you ever got from your partner?" · Send: your partner. 😈
10. STREAMING PARALYSIS – Hook: "I've been scrolling for a movie for 47 minutes!" · Dr. Byte: "And what did you choose?" "…the same sitcom. For the 9th time." · Punch: "That's not entertainment. That's a comfort blanket with a laugh track." · Loop: "Okay, tonight I'll pick something NEW." · Q: "How many times have YOU rewatched your comfort show?" · Send: your couch partner.
