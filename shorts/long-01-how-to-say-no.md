**04 🅣** ΣΚΗΝΗ: `A modern office at 4:59 PM, a tired office worker in her 30s already wearing her coat and holding her bag, ready to leave. A colleague with exaggerated puppy eyes leans on her desk holding a huge thick folder and smiling innocently`
ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Situation number one. It's 4:59. Your coat is on. And then a colleague appears: "Can you just quickly..."`

**05 🅢** ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Has this ever happened to you? Here's the problem: "quickly" is never quick. "Quickly" is the most expensive word in any office.`
ΚΙΝΗΣΗ: `he raises one eyebrow and shakes his head slowly`

**06 🅢** ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `The trick: never answer immediately. Say: "Let me check my schedule and get back to you." You just bought yourself time to think.`
ΚΙΝΗΣΗ: `he taps his temple with one finger, then gives a thumbs up`

**07 🅣** ΣΚΗΝΗ: `The same office worker in her coat calmly checks the calendar on her phone while the colleague with the thick folder waits, then the colleague shrugs and walks away to another desk, and the worker happily walks out of the office into the evening`
ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `And nine times out of ten? They find someone else. Or they suddenly realize it wasn't that urgent after all.`

### 2️⃣ Ο ΦΙΛΟΣ ΜΕ ΤΑ ΔΑΝΕΙΚΑ
**08 🅣** ΣΚΗΝΗ: `A cozy café, a friend in his 30s with a huge charming smile holds up an empty open wallet and puts his hands together begging, while the young man across the table looks suspicious, holding his coffee`
ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Situation two. The friend who needs money "just until Friday". Which Friday? Nobody knows. Possibly a Friday in 2031.`

**09 🅢** ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Has this ever happened to you? Here's my rule: only lend what you could also give as a gift. If losing it would hurt, don't lend it.`
ΚΙΝΗΣΗ: `he holds up one finger like a teacher, then crosses his arms`

**10 🅢** ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Want to help without lending? Say: "I can't lend money, but I can help you find another solution." Kind. Clear. Done.`
ΚΙΝΗΣΗ: `he puts a hand on his heart, then makes three short chopping gestures on the words kind, clear, done`

**11 🅣** ΣΚΗΝΗ: `The same two friends in the café: the young man shows his friend a budgeting app on his phone, the friend first looks disappointed, then laughs and they fist-bump`
ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `True friends understand a no. If someone only loves you when you say yes... that's not a friend. That's a subscription.`

### 3️⃣ ΟΙ ΕΝΟΧΕΣ ΤΗΣ ΟΙΚΟΓΕΝΕΙΑΣ
**12 🅣** ΣΚΗΝΗ: `A family Sunday lunch table full of food, a dramatic mother puts her hand on her heart and looks to the sky, the whole family slowly turns and stares at the young woman sitting at the end of the table, who freezes with her fork in the air`
ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Situation three. Family. "After everything I've done for you..." The most powerful sentence ever invented. Stronger than any lawyer.`

**13 🅢** ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Has this ever happened to you? The trick is called the broken record. You repeat one calm sentence. Again. And again. No explanations.`
ΚΙΝΗΣΗ: `he makes a circular rewind gesture with his finger and smiles calmly`

**14 🅢** ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Like this: "I love you, and I can't come this weekend." "But..." "I love you, and I can't come this weekend." Smile. Repeat.`
ΚΙΝΗΣΗ: `he acts it out playfully, switching between a sweet calm face and a dramatic face`

**15 🅣** ΣΚΗΝΗ: `The same family table: the young woman smiles calmly and repeats herself, the dramatic mother tries several dramatic poses, then finally sighs, laughs and hugs her daughter`
ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Guilt needs arguments to survive. No arguments? No fuel. The conversation ends, and everybody still loves each other.`

### 4️⃣ ΤΟ ΑΦΕΝΤΙΚΟ ΤΟ ΣΑΒΒΑΤΟ
**16 🅣** ΣΚΗΝΗ: `Saturday night, a relaxed man in pajamas on his sofa with a big bowl of popcorn watching a movie, his phone on the table suddenly lights up and buzzes, he freezes with popcorn halfway to his mouth`
ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Situation four. Saturday, 9:47 PM. Your phone lights up. Your boss: "Quick question..." Spoiler: it is never a quick question.`

**17 🅢** ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Has this ever happened to you? Don't just say no. Offer a better yes: "I can't this weekend, but I'll do it first thing Monday."`
ΚΙΝΗΣΗ: `he wags his finger, then opens his hands like offering a gift`

**18 🅢** ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `You're not refusing. You're scheduling. Bosses love a plan. And you keep your weekend, your popcorn and your sanity.`
ΚΙΝΗΣΗ: `he mimes eating popcorn happily and winks`

**19 🅣** ΣΚΗΝΗ: `The same man in pajamas types a short reply, turns the phone face down on the table, smiles, and happily goes back to his popcorn and movie`
ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `And if the boss writes again at 10 PM? That's not a quick question anymore. That's a lifestyle. And not yours.`

### 5️⃣ Ο ΓΕΙΤΟΝΑΣ
**20 🅣** ΣΚΗΝΗ: `An apartment doorway: a cheerful neighbor rings the bell three times in a row, first holding a watering can, then holding a fluffy cat, then standing next to a smiling grandmother with a huge suitcase, while the woman at the door looks more and more shocked`

**21 🅢** ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Has this ever happened to you? Here's the trick that works on everyone: stop explaining. Every excuse is an invitation to negotiate.`
ΚΙΝΗΣΗ: `he holds up both hands like stop signs, then leans in seriously`

**22 🅢** ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `Not: "I can't, my dentist, my cousin, my goldfish..." Just: "Sorry, that doesn't work for me." Full stop. No is a complete sentence.`
ΚΙΝΗΣΗ: `he babbles the excuses fast and silly, then becomes calm and confident for the last sentence`

**23 🅣** ΣΚΗΝΗ: `The same apartment hallway: the woman kindly says a short sentence and closes her door with a smile, the neighbor shrugs, and immediately rings the bell of the next door with the cat and the grandmother`
ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ: `And the neighbor? Finds another neighbor. They always do. You were never the only option. You were just the easiest one.`
