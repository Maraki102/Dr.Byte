# Dr. Byte – 10 θέματα v2 (πιο αστεία)
Κάθε θέμα = 2 κλιπ × 10" · Κλιπ 1 μιλάει ΜΟΝΟ ο ασθενής · Κλιπ 2 μιλάει ΜΟΝΟ ο Dr. Byte
(ό,τι «απαντάει» ο ασθενής στο κλιπ 2 = σιωπηλή αντίδραση ή γραφικό μηνύματος στο μοντάζ)

1. QUICK-QUESTION TRAUMA
K1: "My boss emailed at 3:47 AM. Subject: 'Quick question.' I've already written my resignation letter… and my will."
K2: scans → "Quick question, no emoji, 3 AM… Diagnosis: you're getting promoted. To unpaid overtime." · reads rest: "…He wants the Wi-Fi password." · his own phone buzzes: "From: MY boss. 'Quick question.'" → "…I quit."
Loop → "My boss emailed at 3:47 AM." · ❓ What time did YOUR boss send the worst one?

2. VOICE-MESSAGE POISONING
K1: "My mom sent a SEVEN-minute voice message. Last time it was nine… about a cousin I've never met."
K2: plays it at 10x, hair blown back like a wind tunnel, chipmunk squeaking → "Summary: 'Call me.' Plus the weather. In three cities. Plus the neighbor's hip surgery." · smirks, holds phone like a walkie-talkie: "Don't call her. Reply with an EIGHT-minute voice message. Revenge."
Loop → new voice message pops up: 11:42 · ❓ Longest voice message you ever got?

3. GROUP-CHAT TRIP SYNDROME
K1: "Our group chat has been planning Ibiza since 2019! This year it's REAL!"
K2: scans → "4,312 messages. 96 memes. 14 polls. Flights booked: zero." · "You're not planning a trip. You're running a book club with no book." · types in the chat: "I'm in!!!" … and instantly leaves the group.
Loop → "Since 2019!" · ❓ How long has YOUR group been 'planning'? · 📲 Send to your group chat

4. CHRONIC MONDAYITIS
K1 (Sunday 11 PM, eating pizza in gym clothes): "Tomorrow: 5 AM gym. Salad. Cold showers. NEW ME!"
K2: unrolls a calendar ribbon across the room → "You said that on 47 Mondays. Your gym sent you a 'get well soon' card." · "Diagnosis: the most consistent beginner in human history." · alarm rings 5:00, she (silent) slaps snooze; he quietly takes her last pizza slice: "Next Monday. Same."
Loop → "Tomorrow: NEW ME!" · ❓ What are YOU starting on Monday?

5. 2 AM CART SYNDROME
K1 (buried under boxes): "I don't remember ordering ANY of this! Who did this?!"
K2: unboxes → "A heated toilet seat. A life-size cardboard alpaca. Three air fryers. And a book: 'How to Stop Impulse Buying'… still in plastic." · doorbell, one more box; he opens it, delighted: "Ah. That one's mine." pulls out a FOURTH air fryer.
Loop → doorbell again · ❓ Weirdest thing you bought at 2 AM?

6. VACATION EMAIL DISORDER
K1 (beach chair, sunglasses, laptop): "I'm on vacation! I'm just checking ONE email!"
K2: "You checked 212. You replied 'Thanks, noted!' to a newsletter. And you CC'd your mom." · buries the laptop in sand: "Out. Of. Office." · she (silent) digs it up like a dog; he reads the screen over her shoulder, horrified: "…She's replying to the sand."
Loop → "just ONE email!" · ❓ Do YOU check emails on vacation? Be honest.

7. ADULT FRIENDSHIP SYNDROME
K1: "My best friend and I said 'Let's catch up soon!'… in 2023."
K2: two calendar holograms collide → "Next date you're both free: March 14th, 2029. 4:15 to 4:30 PM. Bring snacks." · "Diagnosis: you love each other deeply. You will never meet again." · her phone buzzes; he types "Thursday?" — she (silent) screams and throws the phone across the room. He nods: "Too soon. Classic."
Loop → "Let's catch up soon!" · 📲 Send to the friend you'll 'see soon'

8. 3 AM CRINGE REPLAY
K1 (exhausted in bed, eyes snap open): "It's 3 AM. Why is my brain showing me THIS?!"
K2: hologram replays: waiter says "Enjoy your meal!", patient answers "You too!" (2009) → "Your brain has one playlist: 'Cringe Classics'. Shuffle on. No skip button. Autoplay at 3 AM." · tries to delete it with his robotic finger, glitch → a NEW memory plays (her waving back at someone who waved at the person behind her): "…Oops. I added one."
Loop → eyes snap open again · ❓ What's YOUR 3 AM cringe memory?

9. FOUR-WORD PANIC
K1: "He texted 'We need to talk'! I've already deleted our photos, moved out… and adopted a cat!"
K2: CODE RED, red lights → reads phone: "'We need to talk… about whose turn it is to buy milk.'" · relieved; phone buzzes again, he reads, slowly: "'Also… why is there a CAT in our apartment?'"
Loop → "He texted 'We need to talk'!" · ❓ Scariest text YOU ever got? · 📲 Send to your partner 😈

10. COMFORT-SHOW ADDICTION
K1: "I've been scrolling for a movie for 52 minutes!… I'm watching the same sitcom. For the 11th time."
K2: "Diagnosis: you know every line. You say them BEFORE the actors." · "Prescription: something new." clicks a new show… 3 seconds later, clicks back: "I don't like change." · camera reveals him on the couch with popcorn, mouthing the lines.
Loop → "52 minutes!" · ❓ How many times have YOU rewatched your comfort show?
