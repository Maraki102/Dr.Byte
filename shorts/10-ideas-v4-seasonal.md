# Dr. Byte – 10 θέματα v4 (εποχιακά + καθημερινά, για Flow 3 πλάνων)
Δομή: Πλάνο 1 ασθενής (8s) → Πλάνο 2 Dr. Byte πετάγεται (10s) → Πλάνο 3 ανατροπή (8s) → 🩺 REAL PRESCRIPTION → ❓ → FOLLOW → loop

## 1. 🛍️ BLACK FRIDAY
- Ασθενής: "I saved 400 euros on Black Friday!" (περιτριγυρισμένη από κούτες) "...I spent 900."
- Dr. Byte (πετάγεται από κούτα): "Congratulations! You didn't save money. You paid for the privilege of a discount!"
- Ανατροπή: έρχεται κι άλλος courier: "Ma'am, there are 12 more boxes."
- 🩺 Real: Make a list BEFORE the sale. Not on the list = not a deal.
- ❓ What did YOU buy on Black Friday that you never used?

## 2. 🎅 SECRET SANTA
- Ασθενής: "I spent 50 euros on my Secret Santa gift. I got... a used candle."
- Dr. Byte (από το κερί): "Diagnosis: you didn't get a gift. You got someone's spring cleaning!"
- Ανατροπή: ο συνάδελφος: "Oh, you got my candle? I got it from my aunt last year."
- 🩺 Real: Agree on a fixed budget out loud. Awkward for 5 seconds, fair forever.
- ❓ What's the WORST Secret Santa gift you ever got?

## 3. 🦃 CHRISTMAS DINNER QUESTIONS
- Ασθενής: "Every Christmas my aunt asks: 'So... when are you getting married?'"
- Dr. Byte (από τη γαλοπούλα): "Easy. Ask her back: 'So... when are you getting divorced?'"
- Ανατροπή: η θεία: "...How did you know?" (σιωπή στο τραπέζι)
- 🩺 Real: Prepare one calm, funny answer and change the topic. You don't owe a life update.
- ❓ What question does YOUR family ask every Christmas?

## 4. 📱 "I'LL JUST CHECK ONE THING"
- Ασθενής: "I opened my phone to check the time. That was 2 hours ago. I still don't know the time."
- Dr. Byte (από την οθόνη): "Diagnosis: your phone didn't steal your time. You gave it away. Voluntarily."
- Ανατροπή: πάει να δει την ώρα πάλι... ειδοποίηση TikTok... "Just one video."
- 🩺 Real: Put a real watch on. Check the time without unlocking the trap.
- ❓ How many hours did YOUR "quick check" take?

## 5. 🍕 "I'M ON A DIET"
- Ασθενής: "I'm on a diet. I only eat salad. ...And then a pizza at midnight. As a reward."
- Dr. Byte (από το κουτί της πίτσας): "Diagnosis: that's not a diet. That's a salad with a pizza subscription!"
- Ανατροπή: ο ντελιβεράς: "Same as every night, Anna?"
- 🩺 Real: Don't starve all day. Eat proper meals and the midnight monster stays asleep.
- ❓ What's YOUR midnight "reward"?

## 6. 🛏️ THE SNORING
- Ασθενής: "My husband snores like a tractor. I haven't slept since 2019."
- Dr. Byte (από το μαξιλάρι): "Diagnosis: you're not married. You live next to a construction site with a wedding ring!"
- Ανατροπή: ο σύζυγος ξυπνάει: "I don't snore." (ροχαλίζει μέσα στη φράση)
- 🩺 Real: Snoring can be a health issue (sleep apnea). A doctor visit can save BOTH your nights.
- ❓ Who snores louder in YOUR house?

## 7. 💬 THE FAMILY GROUP CHAT
- Ασθενής: "347 unread messages in the family group. All 'Good morning' GIFs with flowers."
- Dr. Byte (από το κινητό): "Diagnosis: it's not a family chat. It's a botanical garden with Wi-Fi!"
- Ανατροπή: νέο μήνυμα από τη μαμά: "Why don't you answer? Are you alive?"
- 🩺 Real: Mute the group, not the family. Call one person a week instead.
- ❓ How many unread messages does YOUR family group have?

## 8. 🏠 THE LANDLORD
- Ασθενής: "The heater has been broken for 3 weeks. My landlord said 'wear a sweater'."
- Dr. Byte (από το καλοριφέρ, τουρτουρίζει): "Diagnosis: you don't rent an apartment. You rent a fridge with a sofa!"
- Ανατροπή: SMS landlord: "Reminder: rent goes up next month :)"
- 🩺 Real: Report repairs in writing, with dates and photos. Written beats "I told you".
- ❓ What's the WORST thing YOUR landlord ever said?

## 9. 🙂 "I'M FINE"
- Ασθενής (με ψεύτικο χαμόγελο, όλα καίγονται πίσω της): "I'm fine. Totally fine. Everything is fine."
- Dr. Byte: "Diagnosis: 'fine' is not a feeling. It's a loading screen!"
- Ανατροπή: πέφτει το ράφι πίσω της. "...Still fine."
- 🩺 Real: Name the real feeling: tired, angry, sad. Naming it makes it smaller.
- ❓ How many times did YOU say "I'm fine" today?

## 10. 📦 THE PARCEL "DELIVERY ATTEMPT"
- Ασθενής: "I stayed home ALL day for my parcel. Notification: 'We tried to deliver. You were not home.'"
- Dr. Byte (από το χαρτάκι ειδοποίησης): "Diagnosis: you were home. The courier was not. He delivered the note from his car!"
- Ανατροπή: βλέπει από το παράθυρο το βανάκι να φεύγει γρήγορα.
- 🩺 Real: Use a parcel locker or pickup point. Your day is worth more than waiting.
- ❓ Has YOUR courier ever "tried" to deliver?
