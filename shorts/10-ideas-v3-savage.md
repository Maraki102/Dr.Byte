# Dr. Byte – 10 θέματα v3 (καυστικά, με REAL PRESCRIPTION)
Δομή: hook → χτύπημα → ανατροπή → 🩺 REAL PRESCRIPTION → ❓ ερώτηση → FOLLOW → loop

## 1. 🔑 THE MOTHER-IN-LAW KEY
- Ασθενής: "His mother has a key to our apartment. She came in at 7 AM… to fold my underwear."
- Dr. Byte: "Diagnosis: you didn't marry a man. You joined a family subscription. And she's the admin."
- Ανατροπή: "Treatment: change the locks." Doorbell: mother-in-law… with the locksmith.
- 🩺 Real: Set boundaries as a couple, not alone. He talks to his mom, you talk to yours.
- ❓ Does YOUR mother-in-law have a key?

## 2. 💍 "I'M NOT READY"
- Ασθενής: "7 years together. He says he's 'not ready for marriage'."
- Dr. Byte: "He's not 'not ready'. He's 'not sure'. Seven years is not a relationship, it's a free trial with no end date."
- Ανατροπή: her ex got engaged to someone else after 4 months. "Oh, he was ready. Just not for you."
- 🩺 Real: Ask once, clearly: "Where is this going?" Their answer is information, not a negotiation.
- ❓ How long did YOU wait for the ring?

## 3. 🏋️ THE GYM MEMBERSHIP
- Ασθενής: "I pay 49 euros a month for the gym. I went in January."
- Dr. Byte: "Congratulations. You're not a member. You're an investor. That gym bought a new treadmill with your guilt."
- Ανατροπή: the gym calls… to ask if she's still alive.
- 🩺 Real: Cancel it. Walk 20 minutes a day for free. Rejoin when you actually go 3 weeks in a row.
- ❓ How much have YOU paid for a gym you never visit?

## 4. 💼 "WE'RE A FAMILY HERE"
- Ασθενής: "My boss said 'we're a family here'… then asked me to work Saturday. For free."
- Dr. Byte: "Correct. In a family, you also don't get paid. Diagnosis: you've been adopted. Unwillingly."
- Ανατροπή: "Ask for a raise." Boss: "Let's talk about it… as a family."
- 🩺 Real: Write down every extra hour you work. Numbers win salary talks, not feelings.
- ❓ What's the worst thing YOUR boss said?

## 5. 💸 SALARY DAY
- Ασθενής: "My salary came in at 9:00. At 9:01 it was gone. Rent, loans, subscriptions…"
- Dr. Byte: "Your money doesn't live in your account. It just visits. Like a tourist."
- Ανατροπή: his own account: -3.20 €. "…Fifty euros. Please. I'm also a tourist."
- 🩺 Real: Pay yourself first: move 10% to savings the second your salary arrives.
- ❓ How many days does YOUR salary survive?

## 6. 😴 THE SNORING
- Ασθενής: "He snores like a lawnmower. He says I'M the one who snores!"
- Dr. Byte: "I recorded you both. Diagnosis: you're BOTH right. You're a duet. A horror duet."
- Ανατροπή: he plays the recording, the neighbor bangs on the wall.
- 🩺 Real: Separate blankets save marriages. Scandinavians figured this out years ago.
- ❓ Who snores in YOUR bed? Be honest.

## 7. 📱 THE DATING APP BIO
- Ασθενής: "His bio said 6'1", loves travel, CEO. In person: 5'6", afraid of planes, works at his dad's kiosk."
- Dr. Byte: "That's not a profile. That's fan fiction."
- Ανατροπή: shows HER profile: photo from 2017, "loves hiking". "…You've hiked once. To the fridge."
- 🩺 Real: Video call before the first date. 5 minutes saves you 3 hours of disappointment.
- ❓ Biggest lie YOU saw on a dating app?

## 8. 💬 THE GROUP CHAT
- Ασθενής: "247 unread messages in the family group chat. 200 are 'Good morning' GIFs from my aunt."
- Dr. Byte: "That's not a chat. That's a hostage situation with flowers."
- Ανατροπή: he mutes it… the aunt calls immediately: "Why did you mute us?"
- 🩺 Real: Mute, don't leave. Check it once a day. Peace and no family drama.
- ❓ How many unread messages are in YOUR family chat?

## 9. 🙂 "I'M FINE"
- Ασθενής: "I told my boyfriend 'I'm fine'. He believed me. Now I'm furious."
- Dr. Byte: "Humans are the only species where 'I'm fine' means 'the house is on fire'. And then they're angry nobody called the fire department."
- Ανατροπή: boyfriend: "Everything okay?" She: "I'm fine." Dr. Byte looks at the camera.
- 🩺 Real: Say what you need in one sentence. "I'm upset because…" People can't read minds. Even AIs can't.
- ❓ Do YOU say "I'm fine" when you're not?

## 10. 🏠 THE LANDLORD
- Ασθενής: "The heating has been broken for 3 weeks. The landlord said 'wear a sweater'."
- Dr. Byte: "Diagnosis: you don't rent an apartment. You rent a very expensive outdoor area."
- Ανατροπή: landlord email: "Rent increase: +80 euros. For the new view." The view is the crack in the wall.
- 🩺 Real: Report problems in writing with photos and dates. Written proof gives you rights.
- ❓ Worst thing YOUR landlord ever said?
